About us

A registered Algerian brokerage you can trust

MR L3ALEM AUTO is an officially registered business based in Djelfa, Algeria, run by Lalem Issam Bachir. We provide advisory and coordination services to help Algerian customers import a car from China under their own name, in line with Algerian law.

We are not the importer — you import under your personal right; we handle the sourcing, supplier relations and logistics so the whole process is simple and reliable for you.
